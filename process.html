Our process

No surprises between the measure and the install.

Most countertop complaints come from things that were never discussed. Here is exactly what happens, and what we need from you at each step.

1. Photos or a phone call

Send pictures of the space and roughly what you want. We give a real range over the phone so you know if the project is in your budget before anyone visits.

2. Free in-home measure

We template the actual cabinets, check level and overhang, and talk through edge profile, cutouts, seam placement and sink style. Takes about 45 minutes.

3. Written exact quote

You get a fixed number, itemized: material, fabrication, edge, cutouts, install and tear out if needed. The number does not move unless you change the scope.

4. Slab selection

Bring your cabinet or floor sample to the yard and pick your actual slab, or let us pull three options that fit your palette. We photograph the slab you approved.

5. Fabrication in our shop

Cut, edge and polish in Laveen. Vein layout is planned before cutting on bookmatched and waterfall work.

6. Install and walkthrough

One crew, one visit. Counters set, seams filled, silicone finished, site cleaned. You inspect before we leave.

Questions we get

Straight answers.

How long does it take?

Template to install is typically 7 to 10 business days once the slab is chosen and the quote is approved. Busy season runs longer, so we give you the actual date up front.

Do I have to move my old counters first?

No. We remove and haul them on most standard jobs. If there is tile or a tricky demo, we quote it separately so there is no surprise.

Can I see the slab before it is cut?

Yes, and we recommend it. You approve the actual slab and the vein layout, and we photograph what you approved.

Quartz or granite?

Quartz for consistent look and low maintenance. Granite for unique natural movement and heat tolerance. For outdoor kitchens we steer you toward materials that survive Arizona sun.

Do you work with builders and designers?

Yes. Trade pricing, 48-hour estimates and priority scheduling. Ask for the trade sheet.

Where are you located?

5800 S 67th Ave in Laveen. The shop is where the work happens, so you are welcome to see it.
